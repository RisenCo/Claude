## 1. Boots: composite toe, size 18

**Steel or composite: get composite.** Both pass the same ASTM F2413 impact and compression test. Composite is lighter (that matters at size 18), doesn't conduct cold, and won't set off metal detectors. It's also what almost every size-18 boot uses. The EH (electrical hazard) rating comes from the sole, not the toe.

**Reality check:** most brands stop at size 14–17. These brands stop below 18: CAT, Wolverine, Timberland PRO, Red Wing, Thorogood, Irish Setter, Brahma. No store stocks 18 on the shelf, so order online or ship to store. Ross has no online store, and off-price stores won't have an 18.

## Discounts and money-saving moves

1. **Boot allowance:** ask your employer or union first. It could cover the whole $144–210.
2. **Walmart:** free shipping at $35+ or free store pickup, no codes. Put the jeans and underwear in one cart to clear $35.
3. **Oddball:** code **SHIPFREE** gives free shipping over $75 (✅ seen on the site banner). Build the 12-pair order to $78.85 to use it.
4. **KingSize:** the email signup pop-up reportedly gives 40% off for 48 hours (⚠️ UNVERIFIED, coupon site). If it works, the 6-pk drops to about $21 plus shipping, which beats Oddball per pair.
5. **Free loyalty programs:**
   - Carhartt Groundbreakers: free shipping, about 5% back in points (⚠️ UNVERIFIED).
   - Dickies Foundry: 500 bonus points (✅). dickies.com is also running a 25–50% off sale (✅).
   - Ariat email signup: 10% off your first order (⚠️ UNVERIFIED).
6. **Cash back through Rakuten:** Ariat 4%, Carhartt 2% (✅ but from a Feb 2026 snapshot). Install Capital One Shopping or Honey to test codes automatically at checkout.
7. **Military / first responder:** Lowe's gives 10% to verified military, which makes the HOSS Carson about $139.50. Boot Barn gives 10% in store.
8. **Timing:** Amazon's October Prime sale (Oct 6–7) is already over, and no Columbus Day workwear sales turned up. Buy the essentials now. Save optional extras like a second pair of Carhartts for Black Friday.

## Open questions that could change the picks
- **Jean inseam.** If it's 36, buy Carhartt 102804 or RIGGS 3WAC5, not Dickies or the Walmart Wrangler.
- **Foot width at 18.** HOSS goes to 6E. Ariat Big Rig is EE only in 18.
- **Wet site?** If yes, get the HOSS Buster Hi (waterproof), not the Carson.
