6. Bar or liquid?

  • Same chemistry. The body wash is potassium cocoate and potassium oleate in water with glycerin: a true liquid soap, no sulfates or synthetic surfactants. It is not a different "cleaner" formula, just the bar's chemistry in a bottle with thickener and conditioner.
  • Per ounce the wash is cheaper. $0.78–$1.07/oz versus $1.20–$1.40/oz for the bar at the same retailers.
  • Per shower the bar is cheaper, if it stays dry. Dr. Squatch says a bar lasts 2–3 weeks of daily use. A 60-day test found a bar lasted 9 days in a wet dish and 21–25 days on a draining soap saver. At $6–$7 a bar that is $0.28–$0.33 per shower dry, or $0.78 wet. A 14 oz bottle runs about 30 daily showers at $12.98–$15, so $0.43–$0.50 per shower.
  • Reviews favor the bar slightly. Bars rate 4.5–4.7 on Amazon, Walmart and Target; the wash rates 4.3–4.6, with "thin," "runny" and "scent fades fast" as the recurring complaints.
  • Highest-ROI purchase: a draining soap dish or soap-saver bag. It roughly doubles bar life. Pine Tar also stains tubs and washcloths, which is a cleaning cost.

7. How to buy, cheapest to priciest

  1. Sam's Club or Costco member? Sam's 6-ct $28.98 ($4.83/bar) or Costco.com 5-pack $24.99 ($5.00/bar). Confirm the price in your cart; both were snapshots. Costco warehouse stock was nearly all out as of Aug 2026.
  2. No membership: drsquatch.com at $6.00/bar. Buy 11 bars ($66) to clear the $55 free-shipping line, or set a quarterly subscription of 4+ bars (free shipping, $6.00/bar) and cancel from your account after the first box if you don't want it recurring. The BBB profile shows unanswered complaints about subscriptions, so cancel in the account portal and keep the confirmation.
  3. Need it today: H-E-B Waxahachie ($6.44–$6.98) or Walmart Midlothian ($6.98). Skip CVS and Kroger at $7.29–$7.49 unless a coupon is running (CVS had $3 ExtraBucks for buying 2).
  4. Amazon 10-pack was fetched at $45.00 ($4.50/bar) but a cached snapshot showed $59.99. Check before you trust it.
  5. Try before committing: the official $5 Mini Soap Trial (5 × 2.3 oz, $0.43/oz) is the cheapest Dr. Squatch per ounce anywhere, but it auto-renews at $30 after 30 days.

8. If you want to test a cheaper equivalent

  • Dr. Bronner's Pure-Castile Bar, 5 oz, ~$5.17 (Walmart). Closest formula match: saponified organic coconut/palm/olive, essential oils only, no shea. 4.8 stars on 1,040 Walmart reviews. Consumer Reports' "Best Unscented" and a Wirecutter pick. The $5 experiment.
  • Kirk's Castile, 4 oz, ~$1.49/bar in a 4-pack (Walmart). Five or six ingredients, no palm, no dye. 4.8 stars on 2,554 reviews. 100% coconut soap lathers hard and can feel drying; alternate it if your skin tightens.
  • Every Man Jack Cold Plunge bar, 5 oz, $5.99–$6.99 (Target). The most literal clone: cold-process with shea, kaolin and an exfoliant. But it uses synthetic fragrance with declared allergens plus mica and iron oxide colorants, so it is not simpler.
  • Kirkland Signature Shea bar (Costco), ~$0.67/bar. Consumer Reports' overall top pick for cleaning without dryness. Contains "parfum" and titanium dioxide. Price is a July 2026 tracker figure and the item showed out of stock.
  • Zum Bar, 3 oz, $5.85. Cold-process, superfatted goat's milk, essential oils. Closest to Dr. Squatch's feel in reviews but pricier per ounce ($1.95).

9. Things to know before you buy

  • Unilever owns Dr. Squatch. Announced June 2025; Unilever's audited accounts say 98.7% acquired 2 Sept 2025 for €1,243M. No official formula change has been announced. Complaints of "quality nosedived" exist on Reddit and Trustpilot but are anecdotal, mostly about deodorant.
  • Reviews split on value. Esquire (Mar 2026) called it "Best Cheap Bar Soap for Men." Gear Patrol called $9 a bar "robbery" and said it "disintegrates." The one consistent complaint is that bars melt fast.
  • Sensitive skin: dermatology literature says alkaline true soap can aggravate eczema versus pH-balanced syndets. Since your skin feels better on it, that's your data; Cool Fresh Aloe is the variant reviewers call gentlest.
  • Company ratings: Trustpilot 4.1 (~48k reviews). BBB rating D-, not accredited, for failing to answer 54 complaints, mostly delivery and subscription.
