## 4. Bar vs. liquid

**Chemistry.** The body wash label is: Water, Potassium Cocoate, Glycerin, Naturally Derived Fragrance, Xanthan Gum, Potassium Oleate, Saccharide Isomerate, Citric Acid, Hydrolyzed Gardenia Florida Extract, Ethylhexylglycerin, Hydroxypropyl Guar Hydroxypropyltrimonium Chloride, Maltodextrin, Trisodium Dicarboxymethyl Alaninate ([source](https://www.drsquatch.com/products/fresh-falls-body-wash)). Potassium cocoate and oleate are saponified coconut and oleic fatty acids, so this is true liquid soap, not a sulfate or isethionate wash. The bar is: Saponified Oils of (Sustainable Palm, Coconut, Olive), Naturally Derived Fragrance, Shea Butter, plus scent-specific clays and exfoliants ([source](https://www.drsquatch.com/products/pine-tar)). Both are alkaline true soap.

**Cost per shower** (arithmetic from cited prices and lifespans):

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bar, wet dish | $6–$7 | ~9 | $0.67–$0.78 | 60-day test, [PureBrandReviews](https://purebrandreviews.com/dr-squatch-soap-review/) |
| Bar, draining dish | $6–$7 | 21–25 | $0.24–$0.33 | same test; brand claims 2–3 weeks ([Dr. Squatch](https://www.drsquatch.com/blogs/blog/3-tips-to-extend-the-life-of-your-soap)) |
| Body wash | $12.98–$15 | ~30 | $0.43–$0.50 | ~1 month daily use, [Good Health Academy](https://www.goodhealthacademy.com/reviews/dr-squatch-body-wash/) |

**Reviews.** Bars: 4.5–4.7 stars (Walmart, Target, Amazon). Wash: 4.3–4.6, with recurring complaints "thin," "runny," "scent fades," occasional "fishy" note ([Walmart reviews](https://www.walmart.com/reviews/product/9113509753)).

**Verdict.** Bar plus a draining soap dish. If you will not use a dish, the wash is the cheaper daily driver.

**Shortlist, Dr. Squatch-like for less:**
1. **Dr. Bronner's bar, ~$5.17.** Same oil base and essential-oil scenting, 26% cheaper per oz, Consumer Reports "Best Unscented" and a Wirecutter pick. Lacks shea, so slightly less cushioned.
2. **Kirk's Castile, ~$1.49/bar.** The budget floor, 5–6 ingredients, no palm. 100% coconut soap can feel drying; alternate if skin tightens.
3. **Every Man Jack Cold Plunge, $5.99–$6.99.** The most literal clone (cold-process, shea, kaolin, exfoliant) but uses synthetic fragrance and cosmetic colorants.
4. **Kirkland Signature Shea, ~$0.67/bar.** Consumer Reports' overall top pick; "parfum" and TiO2; price unverified.

**Safety and labeling:**
- EWG Skin Deep: core bars "Low Hazard" (limited data); Pine Tar's allergy sub-score is High due to fragrance and orange oil (data Dec 2022). [link](https://www.ewg.org/skindeep/products/1011295-Dr_Squatch_Mens_All_Natural_Bar_Soap_Pine_Tar/)
- True soap pH 8.5–11 vs syndet 5.5–7; dermatology literature says alkaline soap can aggravate eczema. [Mijaljica et al., Molecules 2022](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8954092/); [Vogue, Apr 2026](https://www.vogue.com/article/body-wash-bar-soap-pros-cons)
- No FDA recall found (search-based, not a database query).
- $9M class settlement over "natural" labeling (Guzman v. Dr. Squatch). $0.50 per product, up to 5 without receipt; claim deadline 27 Nov 2026. Company denies wrongdoing; relabeling as "98–100% natural origin." [settlement site](https://www.personalcareproductssettlement.com/), [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/if-you-purchased-certain-dr-squatch-products-from-november-1-2018-through-august-29-2026-you-could-be-eligible-for-payment-from-a-class-action-settlement-302863248.html)
- NAD (Dec 2023, challenge by Unilever): "no harmful ingredients" claim supported. [link](https://bbbprograms.org/media/newsroom/decisions/dr-squatch-no-harmful-ingredients)
