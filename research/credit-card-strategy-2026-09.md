## 1. Decision (as of 2026-09-15)

### Apply in this order, same day

1. **Amex Blue Cash Preferred (personal).** Use Amex "Apply with Confidence" (soft pull first, shows approval and bonus eligibility before any hard pull). Amex pulls Experian in Texas. Take the $300 / $3,000 in 6 months offer (open in a private window or via referral; some placements show $250).
2. **U.S. Bank Triple Cash Rewards Business.** Pulls TransUnion (unfreeze it first). No U.S. Bank relationship required. Enter true revenue on the application.
3. **Relay Visa credit card (business).** Apply inside the Relay account. No personal credit check. Reports to D&B, Experian Business, Equifax Business.
4. **Request a DUNS number.** Free, up to 30 days. Business name, address, phone must match EIN letter, SOS filing, bank accounts, and card applications exactly.

### Skip for now

- **Chase Ink Cash / Unlimited.** $1,000 bonus (ended 2026-09-17) needed $8,000 spend, unreachable. 2026 Ink denials cluster on: no Chase relationship, LLC under 1 year, low revenue, recent new account. Revisit ~2027-05 or later.
- **Bank of America Business Customized Cash.** Non-customer 3/12 rule; Amex approval makes it 3 new cards in 12 months. $500 bonus needs $5,000.
- **Amex Blue Business Cash.** Amex allows 1 credit card app per 5 days and 2 per 90 days across personal + business. Adds nothing U.S. Bank + Relay do not cover. Optional later.
- **Capital One Spark Cash Select.** Reports full activity to personal bureaus every cycle. Out.
- **PenFed Platinum Rewards.** Points redeem only to gift cards/travel at ~0.85 cents. Rejected on redemption preference.

### Rules after approval

- Do not downgrade or cancel the Amex before 12 months (bonus clawback). At month 12, if annual supermarket spend came in under ~$4,000, product-change to Blue Cash Everyday (no fee, keeps account age). Above $4,000, keep BCP.
- Day 61 on Amex: request 3x credit limit increase online (soft pull).
- Keep Discover (oldest account, ~3 yrs) and the Amazon card open. Put a small recurring charge on the Amazon card so it is not closed for inactivity.
- Pay statement balances in full. Keep reported utilization under 10%.
- U.S. Bank $100 software credit requires 11 consecutive months of a qualifying subscription. Do not let Claude Max lapse or move it.

Break-even BCP vs BCE: ~$1,600/yr supermarket spend covers the $95 fee. At $220-$440/mo ($2,640-$5,280/yr) BCP nets -$16 to +$63 over BCE in steady state; year one BCP dominates because the fee is waived and the bonus is larger.

Walmart, Target, and warehouse clubs do not code as "US supermarkets" on Amex. Aldi does (confirmed).

### 3b. Merchant fee context (verified 2026-09-15)

- Amex merchant cost 2.3-3.5%; Mastercard 1.5-2.6%; Visa 1.4-2.5%; Discover ~1.1-2.5%. Amex runs ~0.3-0.5 points higher on comparable swipes.
- Large supermarkets get discounted tiers: Visa/MC ~1.15-1.40% + $0.05; Amex ~2%.
- On ~$3,960/yr of Aldi spend: Aldi pays Amex ~$79 vs ~$51 on Mastercard. Amex pays the cardholder $238. Amex loses ~$159 on that grocery spend and recovers it via the annual fee, the cap, 1% base spend, interest, and breakage. The cardholder's incremental gain from Amex (~$120) is mostly Amex's money, not Aldi's (~$28-40).
- Aldi is not losing money on an Amex basket: gross margin ~17% on a $50 basket ($8.50) vs ~$1.00 Amex fee. Net margin already prices in a blended card cost.

## 4. Business card research (Be Risen LLC)

### 4a. Category coding

- Anthropic (Claude Max) bills via Stripe as software. Confirmed to trigger the U.S. Bank $100 software credit (Doctor of Credit "what works" list).
- Microsoft 365 does NOT trigger the U.S. Bank credit. Microsoft uses a non-5734 MCC. U.S. Bank confirmed in writing (Travel With Grant, 2023-12, still cited 2026).
- Cloud hosts do not count as "internet services" for Chase Ink Cash 5% (that category is ISPs/telecom). No data points for DigitalOcean, Hetzner, Vercel on the U.S. Bank credit.
- BofA "computer services" 3% covers software, cloud, data processing by MCC. No public data points on Anthropic or Microsoft coding into it.

### 4c. Business credit mechanics (researched 2026-09-15)

- SBFE data feeds FICO SBSS (via Equifax, Experian, D&B, LexisNexis). Bank LOC desks commonly pull SBSS or Experian Intelliscore. D&B PAYDEX matters mostly for vendor/trade credit and needs 2+ tradelines to generate.
- SBA: since 2026-03-01 the SBA no longer requires an SBSS prescreen for 7(a) Small Loans up to $350k; lenders use their own analysis (min 1.1x DSCR). SBA Express may still use SBSS. Personal FICO still dominates for a young single-member LLC; guarantors typically need 650-680+.
- Novo, Relay, Mercury checking accounts do not report anywhere. Only their credit products do (Relay Visa, Mercury IO).
- Cheap net-30 vendors that report: Quill (D&B + Experian, ~90-120 days to first report), Crown Office Supplies (D&B, Experian, Equifax, small fee), Summa (D&B), Uline and Grainger (D&B). Two or three small orders paid early is enough. Optional, not yet done.
- Brex, Ramp, Mercury IO report directly but underwrite on cash balance/revenue. Not a fit for a 2026-05 solo LLC yet.

## 5. Insurance "healthy food" cards (researched 2026-09-15)

Not applicable to a working-age person on a commercial/marketplace plan. These are Medicare Advantage benefits. Since the 2026 plan year, CMS limits food allowances to members with a documented chronic condition (SSBCI) or Dual-Eligible Special Needs Plans. ~11% of general MA plans and ~85% of SNPs offer one; allowances are shrinking into 2027. Networks: Solutran Healthy Benefits+ (Optum), NationsBenefits, InComm OTC Network. Retailers: Walmart and Kroger universal; H-E-B on Solutran/OTC Network; Aldi on NationsBenefits in-store and via Instacart on OTC Network; BJ's not listed; Costco/Sam's plan-specific. Filed only in case it comes up for a parent or client.
